Authors

Institution

SubEdge proposes a subscriber-centric edge computing subsystem for 6G networks that provisions integrated communication and compute resources on a per-subscriber basis. It introduces a computing context binding subscriber identifiers to inference containers and a mobility-event-driven mechanism for joint migration of compute and traffic routing. Experimental results demonstrate reduced latency and high reliability for real-time AI inference workloads during subscriber mobility.

[arXiv260630] Spandana: Reconciling Strict SLOs with Low Cost under Fine-Grained Load Fluctuations

arXiv

Authors
Dilina Dehigama, Shyam Jesalpura, Zeyu Xu, Marton Nemeth, Shengda Zhu, Marios Kogias, Boris Grot
Institution
University of Edinburgh, Imperial College London

Spandana is an architecture that decouples SLO enforcement from cost optimization by steering requests between VMs and a FaaS layer (e.g., AWS Lambda) to handle fine-grained load fluctuations. A lightweight controller per VM ensures SLO compliance, while a resource allocator optimizes VM provisioning based on cost and traffic volatility. The system achieves strict SLO adherence with 76-86% CPU utilization and reduces cost by 5-44% compared to baselines.

Institution

This paper presents a CUDA-based comparative study of GPU parallelization strategies for training shallow neural networks. The authors propose and evaluate three optimizations—tiled shared memory with bank-conflict elimination, pre-transposed weight matrices for coalesced access, and a fused MatMul+ReLU kernel—which together achieve a 1.41x speedup over the baseline CUDA implementation on large datasets, reducing training time from 21.0s to 14.8s on an NVIDIA Tesla T4.

This paper studies the joint problem of function scheduling and data placement in the edge-cloud continuum, formulating it as a Binary Linear Program and proposing centralized and distributed heuristic solutions.
