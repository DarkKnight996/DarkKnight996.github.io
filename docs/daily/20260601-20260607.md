  - **authors:** Yi-Jun Chang, Yang Ze Guan
  - **institution:** National University of Singapore
  - **link:** https://arxiv.org/pdf/2605.30546
  - **Simple LLM Summary:** This paper presents a randomized distributed algorithm for energy-efficient aggregation in multi-hop radio networks, achieving near-optimal energy complexity.
